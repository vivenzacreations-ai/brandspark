Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send my Varmala?

Once you place the order, we share instructions and our address. Courier the flowers in a well-ventilated cardboard box.

What is the processing time?

Resin art requires precision. Please allow 15-20 business days for varmala pieces and 7-10 days for standard decor.

Do you accept returns?

Because every piece is 100% custom-made, we strictly do not accept returns or cancellations once production begins.
