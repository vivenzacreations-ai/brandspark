Frequently Asked Questions

How do I figure out my skin type?

The easiest method is the bare-face test. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser, pat dry, and wait 30 minutes. If your face feels tight, you're dry. If you see shine all over, you're oily. Shine only on the T-zone means combination!

In what order should I apply my skincare?

The golden rule is to go from thinnest to thickest consistency. Cleanse → Toner → Serum → Eye Cream → Moisturizer → Oils. And always finish with SPF during the day!
